# Attribution

Hyprknit is a port of [Window Sweaters](https://github.com/saragordic/window-sweaters)
by Sara Gordić to Hyprland. The knitted renderer, the colourwork charts, the
curated app colourways, the baskets of wool and the gauge all come from that
project and are reproduced here.

Window Sweaters is itself derived from
[JankyBorders](https://github.com/FelixKratz/JankyBorders) by Felix Kratz.

The existing GNU General Public License, version 3, is preserved in LICENSE.

Hyprknit adds the Wayland/Hyprland decoration, a CPU rasterizer standing in for
CoreGraphics, the app-id matching Wayland needs in place of macOS process names,
and the `hyprknitctl` and `hyprknit-swatch` tooling.

Application names in the collection belong to their respective owners and are
used to describe colourway inspiration. No affiliation or endorsement is implied.
